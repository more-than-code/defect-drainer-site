Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what Defect Drainer collects when you use defectdrainer.com and the console at console.defectdrainer.com, why, and what you can do about it. Defect Drainer is run by an individual based in New Zealand (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account identifier, your email address (and whether Google has verified it) and your name. We use them only to sign you in, to show who did what inside your organisation, and to match invites to your email address. We never see your Google password, and we ask Google for nothing beyond sign-in.
  • What you and your team put in. Organisations, projects, repository addresses, defect reports and their screenshots, notes, test plans and runs, fix jobs and their messages, and the names of test accounts you reference.
  • Repository access you grant. If you give Defect Drainer access to GitHub repositories, it uses that access only on the repositories you choose: to open pull requests for fixes and to check whether a fix was merged.
  • Security records. A log of actions taken in your organisation (who, what and when), the time of each sign-in, and request records that hold the route used but not its contents. Our servers and hosting providers also process your IP address to deliver pages and to limit abuse.
  • Cookies. One cookie keeps you signed in, and a short-lived one protects the sign-in itself. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and the site has no tracking scripts. The console keeps a few display preferences in your browser.

How fixes use your data

When you run a fix, the defect's text and screenshots go to the Defect Drainer worker you install on a machine you control, which runs the coding agent you chose there. That agent's provider handles the data under your own agreement with them, not this policy. Agents you connect to Defect Drainer yourself see the defects your organisation lets them see.

Google user data

Defect Drainer's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google account data only to sign you in and identify you in the product. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the providers that host the service for us.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers that run the service for us (cloud hosting, a managed database, and Cloudflare for DNS and content delivery), with Google and GitHub as needed for the features you use, and when the law requires it. Members of your organisation see what is in its projects.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and your organisation's content while the account or organisation exists. A sign-in lasts at most 7 days. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days; copies in backups are deleted as those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing support@defectdrainer.com. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing. You can complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner, or to the data protection authority where you live. Your data may be processed in countries other than yours, where our providers operate.

Children

Defect Drainer is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above, and for a significant change we tell signed-in users before it takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions and requests, email support@defectdrainer.com.
